Sourcing & Vetting

We don't trust a résumé until we've
proven the person behind it.

AI has made fabricated candidates cheap and convincing — including live, AI-assisted interviews. Our pipeline is built for that reality.

Fraud-aware screening
Every résumé is cross-checked against real social proof — LinkedIn history, GitHub contributions, public work — flagging profiles that don't correlate with claimed experience.
Identity verification
Before any client interview, candidates pass a formal ID check: document scanning, database validation, and tamper detection. Background checks for regulated engagements.
Recorded English assessment
Every engineer must articulate architecture and engineering decisions fluently in English. Communication is a non-negotiable, not a nice-to-have.
Channels beyond the job boards
Regional dev communities, open source, referrals, and word of mouth — where the strongest LatAm engineers actually are, and where fraud is hardest to fake.
The hire-bar gate The final filter is our own: “Would we hire this person for our team?” If the answer isn't a confident yes, the profile doesn't reach you. When a candidate does, our name is on it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, clear answers.

Is the transfer in a BOT engagement mandatory?
No — it's an option you hold, not a milestone you owe. Many engagements simply keep running with us. If you do trigger it, scope, notice, and fees are already defined in the original agreement.
Can we convert a contractor to a direct hire?
Yes. Contract-to-hire is a standard path, and conversion terms are set at signature rather than negotiated later.
What happens if an engineer leaves?
We immediately begin sourcing a replacement from our pipeline. Thanks to our retention track record this is rare — but we're prepared when it happens.
What's the minimum commitment?
We recommend a 6-month initial engagement for best results, but there are no long-term lock-ins. Flexible terms are available.
How is intellectual property handled?
All IP belongs to you. Our contracts include full IP assignment clauses and NDAs as standard, executed under US jurisdiction.
What if I need to scale down?
30-day notice to adjust team size. We manage offboarding, final payments, and compliance obligations.
How do you handle performance issues?
You manage performance directly. We provide HR support and can help with coaching, replacement, or transition.
Where will the team be working?
Based on your working policy. Remote is common, and we can add a coworking stipend — or staff a specific city on-site where an engagement requires it.
